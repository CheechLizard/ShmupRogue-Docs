# Story Specification v0.1

## Overview

Narrative framing for the game. Story serves gameplay: lore justifies systems, tone shapes experience.

---

## The Belter

**Who you are:** A cyborg spacer from the outer solar system. Part human, part salvaged machine. You drag ice and minerals back to humanity for a living. Life is radiation, hard vacuum, and jury-rigged parts holding you together.

**Tone:** Antihero. Not saving anyone. Surviving, same as always. Dies constantly, comes back, doesn't give a shit.

**The Corruption:** When you first scan the alien artifact, it does something to your cybernetic systems. You become infected - bound to the artifact. Each death revives you at the artifact's location. You're no longer fully human, no longer fully machine. You're something else now. A replicator, like the probes you're fighting.

**Death Loop Justification:** The roguelike reset isn't abstraction - it's what you are. The artifact won't let you die. Whether this is a gift or a curse remains unclear.

**No Escape:** Space is hostile. Space is infinite. There is no rescue coming. No safe harbor. The warp gates don't lead to salvation - they lead to more space, more probes, more systems being consumed. You're not escaping. You're just buying time, moving deeper into the void, looking for something - anything - that might stop the tide. There is no escape. Only survival.

---

## The Nexari

**What they are:** An alien intelligence, location unknown. Whether biological, synthetic, or something else entirely remains unclear. They've been sending Von Neumann probes across the galaxy for millennia, seeding systems with automated infrastructure. Now they're coming to the solar system.

**The Artifact:** A Nexari beacon/portal device, dormant for millennia. The Glint Scouts found it first - that's why they're here. When you scan it, you accidentally activate it, accelerating their timeline. Now they're building the warp gate. Worse - the artifact bonded to your cybernetics during the scan. You can't destroy it. You can't escape it. You ARE part of it now.

**The Threat:** If the Nexari fleet arrives, hell descends on the solar system. Humanity doesn't stand a chance. Your only option: stop the probes from completing their preparations, buy time, find a way to shut down the beacon.

---

## The Probes

**What they are:** Nexari Von Neumann probes. Fully automated. No crew, no consciousness - just ancient programming executing across the void. They've been drifting toward the solar system for thousands of years.

**Glint Scouts:** The first wave. Small, fast, darting ships that fly in formation. They probe defenses, identify threats, and report back. Individually weak, dangerous in numbers. Named for how they glint in starlight - brief flashes before they're on you.

**Behavior:** Mechanistic, robotic, efficient. They don't hate you. They don't even notice you until you interfere with their protocol.

**The Warp Gate Factory:** The alien artifact you discovered is a matter transformer - a mobile factory that converts raw particles into structures. The probes are using it to build a warp gate. If completed, the Nexari fleet warps in and overwhelms the system.

**Escalation Path:** Level 1 introduces Glint Scouts. Later levels escalate:
- Glint Scouts (Level 1) - reconnaissance, swarm tactics
- Harvester variants - strip-mining resources
- Builder/constructor units - assembling the gate
- Military combat probes - heavy combat units
- The Nexari themselves (endgame?)
